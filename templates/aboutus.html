Our Story

What started as two simple interests, coding and cooking, gradually turned into something we wanted to share with others.

While spending long hours working from home and exploring technology, we also found ourselves experimenting in the kitchen, trying new ingredients, recipes, and flavours. Bringing those two passions together gave birth to THE CODING CHEF, a place where technology meets food.

Our goal is simple: to share easy-to-follow recipes that anyone can prepare at home using everyday ingredients. Whether it's breakfast, rice dishes, vegetarian favourites, or non-vegetarian specialties, every recipe is created with simplicity and taste in mind.

We believe great food doesn't need complicated techniques or hard-to-find ingredients. Good cooking starts with curiosity, creativity, and the willingness to try something new.

So grab your ingredients, explore our recipes, and let's get cooking.
