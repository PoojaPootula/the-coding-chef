⏱️ Preparation Time

30 minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • Potato
  • Oil
  • Mustard
  • Onion
  • Green chili
  • Green peas
  • Tomato
  • Salt
  • Chili powder
  • Turmeric
  • Mutton masala
  • Coriander powder
  • Coriander

👩‍🍳 Cooking Method

In a hot pan, add oil and heat it. Add mustard and let it splutter. Add onions, green chili, and green peas, and saute until soft. Add potatoes and cook until half boiled. Add tomatoes, salt, and turmeric, and cook until the tomatoes become mushy. Add chili powder, coriander powder, and mutton masala with a little water and cook until the oil floats. Sprinkle coriander leaves.

⏱️ Preparation Time

30 minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • Lady fingers
  • Oil
  • Mustard
  • Onion
  • Red chili
  • Garlic
  • Cumin
  • Peanuts
  • Tomato
  • Salt
  • Turmeric
  • Coriander powder
  • Coriander

👩‍🍳 Cooking Method

In a blender, add red chili, cumin, garlic, and salt, then blend into a powder. In a hot pan, add oil and heat it. Add mustard and let it splutter. Add onions and peanuts, and saute until soft. Add lady fingers and cook until half boiled. Add tomatoes, salt, and turmeric, and cook until the tomatoes become mushy. Add the blended chili powder and coriander powder, then cook until the oil floats. Sprinkle coriander leaves.

💡 Special Tips

Keep sauteing the lady fingers on high flame to remove the stickiness. One can add curd also to achieve this.
