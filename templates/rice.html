Rice is a staple food of South India. It is a main dish in most of the homes on the daily menu.

There are more than 5 kinds of rice varieties which are usually combined with some gravy(veg/non-veg) or rasam.

Instead of such combinations, one can make flavored rice which adds nutrient values to the rice, according to the quantity of addition.

Coconut rice is a rarely prepared recipe but a health-packed food. Packed with antioxidant and antibacterial effects, coconut rice makes it a nutrient-filled dish.

⏱️ Preparation Time

10-15 minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Mustard
  • Cumin
  • Peanuts
  • Chana dal
  • Urad dal
  • Dry chili
  • Green chili
  • Coconut
  • Salt
  • Asafoetida
  • Rice

👩‍🍳 Cooking Method

In a hot pan, heat a few drops of oil before adding cumin and mustard seeds. Add peanuts, chana dal, and urad dal. Add red and green chilies, along with curry leaves and asafoetida. Add salt and grated coconut, then add the rice and mix well.

Lemon rice is a go-to recipe when there is leftover rice. It is prepared in many Hindu temples as an offering to deities and to distribute to devotees. It is also widely prepared as a snack when going on a picnic.

⏱️ Preparation Time

10-15 minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Mustard
  • Cumin
  • Peanuts
  • Chana dal
  • Urad dal
  • Dry chili
  • Green chili
  • Lemon
  • Turmeric
  • Salt
  • Asafoetida
  • Rice

👩‍🍳 Cooking Method

In a hot pan, heat a few drops of oil before adding cumin and mustard seeds. Add peanuts, chana dal, and urad dal. Add red and green chilies, along with curry leaves and asafoetida. Add salt and turmeric, then squeeze in lemon juice. Add the lemon juice to the rice and mix well. Check for sourness and add lemon juice as needed.

💡 Special Tips

One can add onions to the dal mixture before adding chilies.

Jeera rice is a savory food when one wants to have a bowl of flavored rice packed with health. Shahi jeera used in jeera rice is usually known for digestion. It is also used to treat coldness like cold or cough.

⏱️ Preparation Time

15-20 minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Shahi jeera
  • Dry chili
  • Green chili
  • Onion
  • Mint
  • Salt
  • Rice

👩‍🍳 Cooking Method

In a hot pan, heat a few drops of oil and add shahi jeera. Add onions, red and green chilies, mint leaves, and asafoetida. Pour in water and sprinkle salt. When the water comes to a boil, add rice while stirring to prevent lumps. Boil the rice until cooked.

Bagara rice is a party food in many South Indian households. It is the first dish one prepares during many functions, parties, and festivals. Accompanying it with any curry makes it even more finger-licking.

⏱️ Preparation Time

20-30 minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • Spices
  • Onion
  • Green chili
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Mint
  • Salt
  • Rice

👩‍🍳 Cooking Method

Add bay leaf, shahi jeera, cloves, cardamom, and cinnamon to hot oil. Add onions, green chili, and salt. Add ginger-garlic paste and fry until the raw smell disappears. Add mint leaves and pour in some water. When the water comes to a boil, add rice that has been soaked for 30 minutes, stirring to avoid lumps. Boil the rice until cooked.

Curd rice is a coolant prepared during summer to beat the heat.

⏱️ Preparation Time

5 minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • Cumin
  • Mustard
  • Peanuts
  • Urad dal
  • Chana dal
  • Curd
  • Red and green chili
  • Oil
  • Curry leaves
  • Asafoetida
  • Rice
  • Salt

👩‍🍳 Cooking Method

In a hot pan, heat a few drops of oil before adding cumin and mustard seeds. Add peanuts, chana dal, and urad dal. Add red and green chilies, along with curry leaves and asafoetida. Add curd and salt to the rice and mix well. Add the seed mixture and mix well.
